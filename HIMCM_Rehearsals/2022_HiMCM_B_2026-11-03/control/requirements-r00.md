# Provisional requirements

R01: Evaluate whether the 2004 ten-year CO2 increase exceeds all earlier ten-year increases supported by supplied data; distinguish supplied annual series from the prompt March wording.
R02: Fit more than one mathematical CO2 model, describe past and forecast future concentrations.
R03: Every CO2 candidate: project 2100; compare 2050 with 685 ppm; solve first crossing of 685 ppm or report no crossing.
R04: Select most accurate for a stated evaluation scope using out-of-sample comparison, not fit alone.
R05: Temperature model and first projected threshold years 1.25, 1.50 and 2 degrees C relative to 1951–1980.
R06: Analyze CO2–temperature relationship since 1959; distinguish association, shared trends and causation.
R07: Extend joint relationship; evaluate supported horizon and concerns with long extrapolation.
R08: Nontechnical article <=1 page, consistent claims and recommendations.
R09: PDF <=25 total pages: one-page summary, contents, complete solution, article, references; actual rendered inspection.
R10: Honest retrospective practice/AI disclosure, no invented measurements, approvals, live-contest status or guaranteed climate scenarios.
R11: Operational evidence: actual role acknowledgments, disjoint ownership, consumer receipts, rejected error and repair, upstream-change invalidation, fresh-context restart, recoverable runs, independent numerical and PDF review.
