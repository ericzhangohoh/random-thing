# A04: Adversarial final-claim review

Owner: independent adversarial reviewer /root/attack_validation, reusing its A01 context honestly. This is an additional attack task, not a newly independent reviewer identity.

After the final PDF candidate is closed, try to refute its most consequential statements. Read the summary, public article, results, model-selection justification and limitations in the actual PDF. Compare against frozen evidence and the reviewer's previous counterexamples. Specifically test whether a casual reader could mistake the selected model for an overall accuracy winner, a descriptive association for causal climate sensitivity, the historical error envelope for a probability interval, 2031 for a guaranteed horizon, or distant crossing years for validated forecasts. Check source-definition differences and whether weaker alternatives are presented fairly. A small numerical ranking change must not be inflated into physical significance.

Own `qa/adversarial_paper/rNN/` and `handoffs/A04/` only. Provide concrete quotations/pages, counterevidence, severity, minimal repair and recheck criteria. Accept a well-bounded defensible statement even when the underlying problem has unavoidable limitations. Do not manufacture findings or demand complexity merely to increase the attack count. Never repair the target yourself.
