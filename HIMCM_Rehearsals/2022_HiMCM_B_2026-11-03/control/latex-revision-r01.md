2. Use numbered sections/equations, restrained tables, legible monochrome plots, citations, references and page numbers. Meet all official requirements and the 25-page total limit, including a one-page summary and one-page nontechnical article.
3. Rewrite connected arguments with varied sentence and paragraph rhythm. Do not change observations, invent findings, add deliberate errors, hide AI use or remove necessary qualifications for stylistic effect.
4. ASCII and fullwidth semicolon counts combined must be <=3 across all rendered paper text. Aim for zero. Verify the actual final PDF, including captions, notes, references and disclosures.
5. Supply a complete sentence register tied to exact source locations and candidate bytes. Include summary, body, captions, notes, article, disclosure and reference prose. Account separately for fragments, equations, table cells and labels. A splitting heuristic must not silently omit content.
6. Three independent reviewers inspect every registered sentence from different perspectives and search for omissions in the actual text. Each supplies a per-sentence ledger with sentence identity, evidence/rationale, verdict and specific repair when needed. Mechanical coverage checks cannot substitute for reading.
7. Authors repair confirmed problems. Reviewers recheck changed sentences and surrounding argument against the final delivered version. Preserve earlier candidates and verdicts. A new mathematical claim requires explicit independent review and acceptance rather than inheriting prior acceptance.
