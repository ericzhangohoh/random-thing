# Pre-probe hypotheses and evaluation strategy
Written before calculating alternative scores. Targets: models/{framing,model_spec,assumptions,model_interface,model_decision,experiment_plan}-r01.md; admitted workbook.
H1: T1 superiority to linear time does not demonstrate CO2 adds predictive information beyond a flexible time trend. Discriminator: quadratic-time OLS using identical training/targets; linear-time OLS on a fixed last-20-year window as a simple recency comparator. Windows are fixed here, not searched. Evaluate development origins 1981,1986,1991,1996,2001 and horizons 1–10 only. Do not score final 2012–2021 or use their alternative forecast errors. Compare MAE and per-origin results against independently implemented existing candidates. An improvement only supports omitted-comparator fragility; no alternative is declared a new primary winner.
H2: log-CO2 relation is trend-sensitive. Independently compute raw, linear/quadratic detrended and differenced correlations using development observations through 2011 only; examine algebraic identity of E-driven T1 to a time-linear path. No causal finding is sought.
H3: requirement/source/threshold semantics may fail. Read complete original PDF via fresh text extraction, compare explicit task definitions and model wording. Annual/March and CO2/CO2-equivalent corrections are source-definition findings, not source-specific empirical claims verified from later data here.
Pin SHA256 of inputs and six target docs. Bound probe to deterministic least squares and stated scores; no new data, no model-owner edits. Final holdout exposure: official full data table is read but no final alternative scores will be computed. Full-series author outputs if subsequently read will be disclosed.
