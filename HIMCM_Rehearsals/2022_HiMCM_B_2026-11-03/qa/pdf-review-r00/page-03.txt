Mauna Loa is an atmospheric indicator, while temperature is a global index; their spatial definitions

Annual observations do not become March observations
The statement's March caption conflicts with its annual-mean source and supplied values. We
interpret the modeling series as annual. It can answer the supported annual comparison but cannot
directly verify or disprove a March-specific record or claims about all earlier history. [1,3]
May 2022 and the OECD benchmark
NOAA's June 2022 release gives May monthly means of 420.99 ppm (NOAA) and 420.78 ppm
(Scripps), supporting rounded 421 ppm. This monthly Mauna Loa context is excluded from fitting
and validation. [4]
OECD's approximately 685 ppm in 2050 denotes greenhouse gases in CO2-equivalent under a
conditional baseline. Its separate CO2-only levels are approximately 530 ppm in 2050 and 780 ppm
in 2100. We retain the requested pure-CO2 exercise without presenting a mismatch as a test of
OECD's different quantity. [6]
