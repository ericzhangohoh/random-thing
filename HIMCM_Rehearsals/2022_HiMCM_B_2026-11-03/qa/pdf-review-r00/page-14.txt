Use the findings without overstating them
Keep measurements comparable. Preserve the annual/monthly distinction, temperature
baseline, data vintage and CO2-equivalent definition before interpreting a quoted number.
Monitor performance and compare assumptions. Preserve the chosen rule for an honest
assessment, track later errors and show competing paths. Ranking reversals and envelope misses
should prompt reassessment, not disappear.
Use more evidence for intervention decisions. Planning can compare conclusions under
different assumptions. Quantifying a policy's effect requires emissions, forcings, sinks and response
dynamics that these two records do not identify.
These recommendations follow from definition conflicts, ranking instability and extrapolation
sensitivity. We do not estimate a specific intervention's benefit or assign probabilities to distant
scenarios.
