The direct answer for selected T1-D is 2036, 2048 and 2076. Alternative concentration
assumptions move the linked dates; T0 gives 2044, 2059 and 2089. The last two columns are
anomalies, not absolute temperatures.
A crossing is the first integer year after 2021 whose unrounded central-path value reaches the
threshold, searched through 2300. It is not the first realized warm year or the start of sustained
warming. T0 in 2088 is 1.999310 C, which displays as 2.00 but is below 2; its crossing is correctly
2089.
No change of baseline or certainty
These dates refer to the 1951-1980 mean, not pre-industrial warming or Paris targets. They are
conditional arithmetic, not probability quantiles, established deadlines or validated century-scale
forecasts.
