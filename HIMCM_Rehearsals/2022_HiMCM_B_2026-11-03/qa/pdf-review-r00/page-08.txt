T0 measures what this specified full-history linear trend predicts. T1 associates equal proportional
concentration changes with equal changes in fitted anomaly. Its slope is not identified climate
sensitivity or a causal policy-effect coefficient.

therefore includes upstream concentration error. An invalid path cannot be rescued with future
observations.
