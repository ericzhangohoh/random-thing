Approach and scope
The question has three linked tasks: check concentration claims, model the
concentration-temperature relationship, and explain the implications to a public audience. We
reconcile definitions, then compare simple continuations with chronological forecast tests.
Concentration outputs become inputs to the linked temperature extension. [1]
All fitted observations come from the supplied historical workbook. Dates answer the mathematical
exercise under explicit continuation assumptions, not an emissions-driven climate model. Tables
and figures use the reviewed scientific run; exact evidence bindings and reproducible sources
accompany this practice report.
