The annual 2004 rise was not the largest
A ten-year interval contains ten elapsed years, so we compare endpoints rather than count ten
listed observations. The increase and annualized rate are
Delta10,t = Ct - Ct-10; g10,t = Delta10,t/10.

The 2004 window ranks second among the 36 supported intervals ending 1969-2004. Its increase is
0.03 ppm below the earlier maximum ending in 2003. Therefore we do not agree that 2004 is
strictly largest in the supplied annual series.
What this answer does not establish
The 0.03-ppm margin is arithmetic, not evidence of a physically significant difference. Annual data do not
settle the actual March assertion, and the supplied coverage cannot test every earlier historical period.
