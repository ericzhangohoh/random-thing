The indicators rise together strongly at level. The log-concentration fit has descriptive R-squared
0.922302. This does not mean CO2 caused 92.2% of warming. Removing separate linear or
quadratic time trends weakens the association; annual changes weaken it further.
Detrending and differencing change the question and remove low-frequency information. Weaker
correlations neither disprove a physical greenhouse mechanism nor identify its magnitude. Omitted
forcings, response delays, serial dependence and the station/global spatial mismatch prevent these
two series from isolating cause.
These are the primary 1959-2021 diagnostics (1960-2021 for changes), not earlier exploratory
values from a shorter period. We report descriptive relationships without independent-observation
p-values or a causal climate-sensitivity interpretation.
