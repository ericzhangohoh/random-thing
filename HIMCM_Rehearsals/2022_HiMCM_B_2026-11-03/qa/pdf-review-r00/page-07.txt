None reaches 685 ppm by 2050. Q first reaches it in 2100, D in 2131 and E in 2136. These are
first integer annual central-path crossings under the fitted continuation, not forecasts validated at
those dates.
Raw boundary pairs (previous year, crossing year) are D: 684.59, 687.05 ppm; Q: 683.85754,
688.32682 ppm; and E: 683.06729, 686.14233 ppm. Each crosses using unrounded values.
The spread grows from 30.52 ppm in 2050 to 104.63 ppm in 2100. Divergence reflects the
absolute-growth, acceleration and proportional-growth assumptions. It is sensitivity across these
models, not a probability interval or bound on all plausible futures.
Source and horizon caution
OECD reports a different quantity, CO2-equivalent. [6] Tests of at most ten-year forecasts cannot validate
these distant values. Numerical digits reproduce model arithmetic, not future measurement precision.
