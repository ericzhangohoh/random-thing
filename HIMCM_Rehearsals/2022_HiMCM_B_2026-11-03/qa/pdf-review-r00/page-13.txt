Strengths, limits and validity checks
What the architecture does well
The models are transparent, use few parameters and represent distinct continuation assumptions.
Evaluation respects information available at each origin. Linked temperature scores include
concentration forecast error. A fixed selector, explicit alternatives, residual diagnostics and adverse
results make the answer inspectable rather than dependent on a single fitted curve.

Mathematical validity is narrower than credibility
All actual full and declared last-30 paths are finite, valid and nondecreasing over the annual search
domain 2021-2300. Reported crossings satisfy unrounded boundaries; the supplied history never
exceeds the requested levels. These facts establish calculation status, not climate credibility
through 2300.
First-passage inference uses only the contiguous valid prefix from the origin. A crossing before later
invalidity is retained with a warning; invalidity before crossing blocks a date. Already-above-origin
and valid non-crossing-through-2300 cases have different statuses. No later valid segment bridges
an invalid gap.
Why not simply add complexity?
High-degree curves add extrapolation flexibility without long-horizon evidence. A logistic capacity
is not identified by this rising record. Physical carbon-cycle and energy-balance models need
emissions, sinks, forcing and response inputs absent from the two supplied series. These justify a
bounded exercise, not an exhaustive-model claim.
