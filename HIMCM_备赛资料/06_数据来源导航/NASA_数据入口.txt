Data.nasa.gov: A catalog of publicly available NASA datasets
What is Data.nasa.gov?
Data.nasa.gov
is NASA's publicly available metadata repository, providing information about datasets related to science, space exploration, aeronautics, and more. In support of the OPEN Government Data Act, making NASA's metadata publicly accessible promotes transparency, collaboration, and scientific advancement.
Data.nasa.gov describes NASA datasets rather than hosting the data itself. Most dataset pages summarize the available data and include links to where it is actually hosted—typically on other NASA archive or data systems. In this way, Data.nasa.gov serves as a central resource for discovering NASA datasets and finding where the underlying data can be accessed.

Open Dataset Resources Tied to Data.nasa.gov
NASA datasets are available through a number of different
                    websites, not just Data.nasa.gov.
Applications & Data Services (ADS) Program
Data.nasa.gov is the dataset-focused site of NASA's OCIO
                    (Office of the Chief Information Officer) Applications & Data Services (ADS)
                    program. There are also API.nasa.gov and Code.nasa.gov for
                    APIs and Code respectively.
Applications & Data Services (ADS)
Code.nasa.gov
A page that contains information on links to all
                                    open-sourced NASA code projects.
Api.nasa.gov
A clearinghouse site for information about NASA APIs.
                                    Acts a passthrough site to NASA APIs elsewhere. Request
                                    a key and get to work!
Data.nasa.gov(this site)
NASA's central open-data site for the public. Aggregates
                                    data from different archives and datasets not available
                                    anywhere else.

Code and data from all US federal agencies
Code.gov and data.gov are aggregator sites run by GSA
                    (General Services Administration) that collect open-data and
                    open-code from across all U.S. federal government agencies.
                    Code.nasa.gov and Data.nasa.gov feed into code.gov and
                    data.gov respectively. Data.gov has an APIs for working with
                    their datasets that make them worth checking out even if
                    you're strickly interested in NASA data.
