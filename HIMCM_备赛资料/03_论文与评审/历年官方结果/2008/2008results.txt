                        RELEASE
2008
11th Annual High School Mathematical Modeling Contest
The
                        Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP)
                        is pleased to announce the results of the Eleventh Annual
                        High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling (HiMCM).
                        A total of 269 teams, with up to 4 students each, from
                        52 schools, competed. All teams worked at their own schools
                        during a designated 36-hour period between November 7th
                        and 24th, 2008. Each high school team chose from two modeling
                        problems offered and then constructed their solutions.
