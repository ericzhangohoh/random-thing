## Operational consequences

Record official work and submission deadlines separately, including their timezone. Current instructions require an English PDF, a 25-page main solution limit, readable type of at least 12 points, appropriate team/page headers and anonymity. Check the original instructions for exact counting, naming, file-size and submission details.

During the contest, interaction is restricted to the registered team, and posting/sharing the problem or work externally is prohibited. A private repository is a storage choice, not an official ruling on a particular collaboration arrangement. Keep live-contest access and activity within the applicable rules.

The AI policy permits responsible use and requires transparent identification of tools and purposes, verification of generated material and sources, citations and an appended AI-use report. Keep actual activity records while working; do not invent them afterward.

The workflow's G1/G2/G3 human checkpoints preserve the student team's substantive responsibility. The source documents remain authoritative for details and future changes.
