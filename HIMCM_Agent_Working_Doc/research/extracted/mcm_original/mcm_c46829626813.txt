        Measure of Inﬂuence and Similarity in Dynamic

                                        Summary




                                        AT      ：
     To measure the evolution and revolution of musicians’ community, our team intro-
  duces a model to quantify their identities across time based on the dynamic networks
  from interactions between musicians across time. The model contains three core parts:
  rescaled PageRank to measure inﬂuence, optimization of weighted cosine distance to




                                                   数
  measure similarity with time structure, and a comprehensive measure combing inﬂu-
  ence and similarity.




                                           Hm
      To measure importance of nodes in network more accurately, we improve the tradi-
  tional PageRank model by introducing a method of "rescaling". This is a method tak-




                                                     学
  ing corresponding nodes’ relative positions among contemporaries into account which
  effectively solves the problem of time preference in the ordinary PageRank and allow
  us to compare inﬂuence of musicians vertically.




                                             od
      Then, based on musical characteristics of their works across time, we design a




                                                      模
  weighted cosine distance as the measure of similarity. And in order to determine the

                         众
  weight vector, we optimize the error function of MSE with its labels from the structure
  of networks by Stochastic Gradient Descent. Except from similarity of musical identi-


                          号
                                               els
  ties from the same period, we also consider the potential lag effects of previous works




                                                        型
  in our model. This makes the similarity model precisely capture musicians within a
  same genre.


                 公
      In addition, we conbime the similarity into rescaled PageRank model in order to
  making use of more information than indegree. With this update, our model can better
  overcome the problem of time-bias. Also, by the comparison of results between two

          信
  measures, we are able to take a glimpse at the relationship between inﬂuence and simi-
  larity. And by evolution of inﬂuence and musical characteristics, we can also trace how


  微
  a genre change over time with different positions in the network. Some cases studies

                                   M
  are presented to show how it works.
      Finally, we made some tests to check whether we can dig out more information


                   号
  from the graph structure by Graph Convolutional Network(GCN). Sensitivity analysis
  is also held to determine the width of average window in the process of rescaling.


                    ：
  Strength and weakness of the model are also summarized for further imporving the
  measures.
