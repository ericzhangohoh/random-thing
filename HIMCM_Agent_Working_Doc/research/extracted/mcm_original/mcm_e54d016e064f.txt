 1.1   Problem Background

     The culture and art industry has witnessed an unprecedented boom recently. Delighted
 with an ample supply of excellent masterpieces of art, audiences around the world are grateful
 for the effort made by those great artists. Among these popular productions, musical pieces
 are the most influential.
