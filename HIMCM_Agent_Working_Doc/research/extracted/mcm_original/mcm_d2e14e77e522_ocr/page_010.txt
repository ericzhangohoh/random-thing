4.2 Numerical Approximation Schemes
The Idea: We have two sets of boundary data.

1. The hydrograph of the discharge of the dam breach gives us the flowrate
and area (Q(zo,t) and A(zo,t)) for all time values at the position of the
dam.

2. We also have the initial conditions on the steady state flowrate at every
position downstream at the particular instant the dam breaches.

We discretize our data in terms of the variables (x;,t,), where x; represents the
position at the i” position on our grid of the river and t, is the n“ time step.
