5 Getting Results

Having modeled the geometry of the river, and collected hydrographs of various
erosion and flow rates, it is time to embark into the depths of flood. Unfor-
tunately the fill capacity of our geometric river model could not accommodate
extreme flood conditions. We found a flood insurance model for the area, and
although their floodplain was exaggerated for a safe evacuation, it was clear
from their model that our river model did not span nearly the amount of land
mass that would be necessary to consider an extreme dam breach [5]. This mod-
elers intuition of the power and devastation that could be caused by a breach
in the dam above the Saluda River was clearly clouded by too much time spend
in front of books, and not enough at the river.
