5.1 Rawls Creek

Our flood model allows us to determine the maximal cross-sectional area of flood
water present where Rawls Creek meets the Saluda River. The flood depth
corresponding to this maximum area is computed using the known riverbed

geometry.
e Initializing a maximal dam breach discharge of 3000 m3/s under condi-
tions of fast erosion we calculate the corresponding flood depth at 12.5

meters. We estimate the flow up Rawls Creek to 3.5km.
e Setting the breach to be a slow eroding breach of discharge reaching max-

imal output at 1000m3/s, the water depth was 8.9 meters. This corre-
sponds to flood waters rising to an elevation which at maximum would

flow a distance of 2.5km up Rawls Creek.
