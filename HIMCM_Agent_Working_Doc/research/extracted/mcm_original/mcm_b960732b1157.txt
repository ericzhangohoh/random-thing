                           Cleaner of the Earth: Fungi
                                        Summary
        Fungi decompose organics into inorganics through their respiration, playing a
  crucial role in the ecosystem. In this paper, in order to discuss their decomposition




                                                  ：
  ability, multiple species interaction, and significance of biodiversity, we establish




                                          AT
  three models: Fungal Decomposition Model, Fungai Propagation Model and Fungal
  Population Competition Model.
        To begin with, we collect the original experimental data in literature. After clean-
  ing off redundant values, we finally obtain 34 fungal samples. Then we examine their




                                                     数
  credibility through conclusions of reference paper offered in requirement. Further, we




                                             Hm
  sort these 34 samples into 3 categories through hierarchical clustering method in light
  of their moisture tolerance and hyphal extension rate. It covers Sprinter, Middle-dis-
  tance runner, and Long-distance runner. Moreover, we put forward 4 potential mod-




                                                       学
  els for fungal decomposition. Then, applying multiple linear regression analysis, we
  obtain Fungal Decomposition Model for multi species after comparison.
        Then, we establish population growth model for single species based on the dif-




                                               od
  ferential equation. Further, we obtain the Fungal Propagation Model by introducing




                                                        模
  a relative environmental impact factor. Besides, we build Fungal Population Compe-

                          众
  tition model. Likewise, we quantify the interaction between fungi and environment
  by the introduction of relative environmental impact factor. Based on these models,


                           号
  we conduct simulations and find that multiple species community’s population scale




                                                 els      型
  is 1.5 times that of single species and its decomposition rate will be 1.45 times on the
  condition of optimal environment and same initial proportions.

                  公
       Next, we clarified the concrete form of environmental fluctuations. We assume
  that moisture in environment and temperature change periodically like trigonometric
  functions. Further, combining the models we established before, we analyze the sen-

          信
  sitivity of fungal population scale and decomposition rate to rapid fluctuations in the
  environment both in short-term and long-term. We find that fungi, especially multiple
  species community, are susceptible to environmental fluctuations in the short term

  微                                 M

  tiple species community’s population scale will be 1.18 times that of single species and

       Besides, we predict the performance of 4 combination of multiple species in differ-
  ent environment respectively, which includes arid, semi-arid, temperate, arboreal,
