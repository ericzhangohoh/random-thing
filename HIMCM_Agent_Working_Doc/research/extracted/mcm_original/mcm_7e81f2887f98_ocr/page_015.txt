6.3 Stability Check

In order to guarantee that the result of simulation doesn’t shift too much when
the inputs change slightly, we make the following variations on the inputs and
monitor the output of the simulation:

e We vary the occupancy of the park by + 200 people. The average utility
varies less than 2%.

e We slightly change the score list of the attraction set by 1% to 4%, but
keep the ratio among each ride score. The output shows no significant
difference.

e We change the group size of users to 1, 2, and 5. The result only changes
by tiny amounts.
