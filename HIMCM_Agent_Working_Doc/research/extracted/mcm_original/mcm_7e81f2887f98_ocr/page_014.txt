6 Park Simulation

6.1 Assumptions

Some of the interactions between real park users and real parks are too compli-
cated to simulate in a computer. We therefore make the following assumptions
to simplify the problem:

e The park distribution is simple. Topoland opens for 12 hours a day.
The 6 hours in the middle are considered to be busy hours. Park users
arrive and leave sequentially in the first and last three hours, rather than
rush in and out simultaneously. The park occupancy distribution assumed
is depicted in Figure 3.

e Park users act in groups of 10. This reduces the computation time by
a great factor, and the effect of the clustering tends to average out when
the number of park users is large.

e Park users spend minimal time walking and always use their
QuickPasses.

e Each park user has the same User’s Patience and User’s Idle
Constant These are set to the average values obtained in Section 4.3.

e Each attraction in the park is represented by a real queue and a virtual
queue. The real queue holds the park users who wait in the normal line,
