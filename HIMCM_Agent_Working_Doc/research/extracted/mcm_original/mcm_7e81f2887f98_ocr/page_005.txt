2 Introduction

After the introduction of the popular Mébius Madness Roller Coaster, which
circles the track twice to return riders right side up, the waiting times at the
Topoland amusement park became an unpleasant two hours or more. To reduce
waiting time, Topoland developed and implemented a QuickPass scheme.

In a QuickPass scheme, a customer may decide not to wait in the long line for
an attraction and instead insert their entrance ticket into a machine near the
attraction. The machine gives the customer a QuickPass that allows him or her
to return at a given later time and wait in a second line which is presumably
quite short.

The QuickPass scheme currently used by Topoland has several flaws. The sys-
tem may give a pass with a return time of four hours but then shortly thereafter
give a pass with a return time of only one hour. Also the lines for people with
passes can be as long as the normal line.

In this report we compare various QuickPass systems and recommend a Quick-
Pass system to replace the current one used by Topoland. Our task is the
following:

1. Propose various QuickPass schemes.
2. Determine the criteria we will use in evaluating our proposed schemes.

3. Develop and implement a model to evaluate these schemes according to
our determined criteria.
